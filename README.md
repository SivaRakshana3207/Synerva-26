# SYNERVA '26

## National Level Technical Symposium

SYNERVA '26 is a National Level Technical Symposium organized by the
**Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Arunachala College of Engineering for Women**.

It brings together technology, creativity, coding, innovation, and competition
to provide students with an exciting platform to showcase their technical skills.

## 🚀 Events

SYNERVA '26 features five exciting technical and creative events.

### 1. THOUGHT SPHERE

**Technical Paper Presentation**

Present your innovative technical ideas through a concise paper presentation
and engage with the judges during the interactive query session.

### 2. CODE RELAY

**Relay Programming Challenge — Java**

A relay-based programming challenge where teammates take turns working on
a single program and continue from where their teammate left off.

### 3. TECH DOMINO

**Clue Matching Challenge**

Match clues with their corresponding answers, arrange the sequence correctly,
and identify the cards that do not belong.

### 4. GAME REEL

**Game Intro Video Editing**

Create a creative and engaging **30-second to 1-minute game introduction or trailer**
using the provided game assets.

### 5. WEBBOT

**Web Development & Chatbot Creation**

Build a working website and chatbot based on a surprise topic,
on the spot and within the given time limit.

## 🏫 About the Department

The **Department of Computer Science & Engineering** at Arunachala College
of Engineering for Women focuses on developing strong technical knowledge,
creativity, innovation, and industry-ready skills.

Through hands-on learning, technical events, workshops, projects, and
collaborative experiences, students are encouraged to become confident
problem solvers and future technology leaders.
